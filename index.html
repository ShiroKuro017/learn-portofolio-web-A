About Me

Hello! I'm Muhammad Wira Widhana, also known as ShirO, a 2D/3D Generalist and Multimedia Generalist focused on 3D assets and 2D illustration character portraits with an anime style.

I create 3D assets for games and animation, from characters to props, with full texturing. For 2D illustration, I focus on concept characters with an anime aesthetic. My main goal is to create art that delivers strong visuals and meaning to its audience.
